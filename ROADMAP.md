# Business Snapshot Roadmap

## Current Working Concept

The configured brand is a business snapshot tool for small businesses. It helps reveal the online details that quietly cost calls, orders, quote requests, visits, and bookings.

The product is the report. The service is fixing what the report exposes.

## Core Promise

Help small businesses clean up the online details customers check before choosing them:

- Google Business Profile accuracy
- hours of operation
- phone, address, and service area consistency
- website clarity and trust
- services, offers, or menu accuracy
- photos and visual proof
- reviews and review generation
- maps visibility
- quote, call, booking, catering, or ordering paths

## Monetization Path 1: Service First

Start by selling a one-time Local Presence Cleanup package.

Potential price range: $497-$1,500.

Possible deliverables:

- Business Snapshot report
- Google Business Profile cleanup
- hours, phone, address, service/menu accuracy check
- website basics check
- photo cleanup or upload guidance
- improved service/menu descriptions
- review request link or simple review process
- quote/order/contact path fixes
- before/after score report

Example positioning:

"I check the places customers look before choosing you, show what may be costing calls or orders, then clean up the biggest issues."

## Monetization Path 2: Monthly Maintenance

Offer recurring support after the initial cleanup.

Potential price range: $99-$299/month.

Possible monthly deliverables:

- monthly business snapshot
- holiday/special hours updates
- Google profile photos/posts
- service/menu updates
- review count/rating monitoring
- review response drafts
- broken link/order/contact flow checks
- monthly score report

## Monetization Path 3: Free Report Funnel

Use the app as a lead-generation wedge.

Process:

1. Pick one niche or neighborhood.
2. Run 10 manual business snapshots.
3. Send a short message offering the business snapshot.
4. If they reply, send the report.
5. Offer a paid cleanup.

Example outreach:

"I noticed a few online details that may be costing you customers, like hours/listing/menu/review/contact issues. I made a quick business snapshot for your business. Want me to send it?"

## Monetization Path 4: Productized Packages

Potential packaging:

- Basic Cleanup: $497
  Google profile, hours, phone/address, service/menu basics.

- Growth Cleanup: $997
  Basic + website fixes + photos + review request system.

- Full Local Presence Setup: $1,500-$2,500
  Website refresh + Google profile + listings + lead/order flow.

## Longer-Term SaaS Possibilities

Only explore this after people pay for the service.

Possible products:

- $19-$49/month small business self-monitoring
- $49-$199/month freelancer/agency report generator
- Google Places API auto-fill
- Google Business Profile integration
- review monitoring
- menu/photo/listing checks
- recurring report emails
- client portal
